Three events that shaped our major

Written by Tomás Martínez, Felipe Mora, David Moreno, Luna Rodriguez and Valentina Zarate

Event 1: The creation of the Internet – 1983

In 1983, the Internet started to use a system called TCP/IP. This was an important moment because computers could communicate with each other in a better way. Today, the Internet is very important for technology. Data engineers use the Internet to send, receive and work with a lot of information. Without the Internet, many of the technologies that we use today would be very different.

Event 2: The creation of the World Wide Web – 1989

In 1989, Tim Berners-Lee created the World Wide Web. The Web allowed people to access information through websites. This changed the way people use computers and information. Today, we can use websites to study, work, buy products and communicate with other people. For our major, the Web is also important because we can create applications that work with data and artificial intelligence.

Event 3: The growth of Artificial Intelligence – 2010s

During the 2010s, Artificial Intelligence became more popular and powerful. Computers started to learn from large amounts of data and could do more complex tasks. Today, AI can help people with images, text, data and many other things. This event is important for our major because we study both data and artificial intelligence. In the future, AI will probably be part of many different jobs and technologies.

Why we chose to study our major

Written by Tomás Martínez, Felipe Mora, David Moreno, Luna Rodriguez and Valentina Zarate

We chose to study Data Engineering and Artificial Intelligence at Universidad Santo Tomás for different reasons. First, we think this career can give us many job opportunities in the future. Today, many companies need people who can work with data, technology and artificial intelligence. Because of this, we can work in different areas and companies.

Another reason is the money. Technology is an important area and many technology jobs can have good salaries. We know that money is not everything, but it is important for our future. We want to have a good job and a stable life. With this career, we can work in areas like data analysis, programming, databases, artificial intelligence and software.

We also chose this major because we had already seen some similar things at school. Some of us worked with computers, programming or technology before. Because of this, the major was not completely new for us. We liked learning about technology, so we wanted to continue learning more at university.

We also like that this career can have many different paths. We could work in a company, create our own projects or work remotely. In the future, we could work with international companies and use English every day. We will need to learn more programming, mathematics and technology, but we think it will be useful for our future.

Finally, Artificial Intelligence is growing very quickly. We think it will change many jobs and industries. As students of this career, we can learn how these technologies work and how to use them. For all these reasons, we decided that Data Engineering and Artificial Intelligence is a good career for us.

Advantages and disadvantages of our major

Written by Tomás Martínez, Felipe Mora, David Moreno, Luna Rodriguez and Valentina Zarate

Advantages

One advantage of our major is that there are many job opportunities. Data is important for almost every company, so data engineers and AI professionals can work in different areas. We can work in technology, finance, health, marketing, business and other industries.

Another advantage is that we can work with new technologies. We learn about programming, databases, data and artificial intelligence. These technologies are growing, so we can learn new things during our careers. We can also work remotely in some jobs, which can be useful in the future.

A third advantage is that this career can have good salaries. Companies need people with technology skills, so these jobs can offer good economic opportunities. We can also create our own projects and businesses with the knowledge that we learn at university.

Disadvantages

One disadvantage is that the career can be difficult. We need to study mathematics, programming and other technical subjects. Sometimes the topics can be confusing, and we need to practice a lot to understand them.

Another disadvantage is that technology changes very fast. We cannot stop learning after finishing university. We will need to learn new programming languages, tools and technologies. This can take a lot of time.

Finally, working with computers for many hours can be tiring. We can spend a lot of time sitting and looking at a screen. For this reason, we should take breaks, exercise and have a good balance between university, work and personal life.
